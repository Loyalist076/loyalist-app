Loyalist Closes Purchase of the Loveland Project and Announces $275,000 Financing

Toronto, Ontario – June 30, 2025 – Loyalist Exploration Limited (CSE: PNGC) is pleased to announce it has completed the acquisition of the Loveland gold, copper, and nickel Property from STLLR Gold Inc. The property, located 45 km northwest of Timmins, Ontario, is the first acquisition under the Company’s “Buy and Explore Timmins” strategy.

Property Highlights

  • 292 contiguous mineral claims totaling 6,244 hectares (15,430 acres)
  • Hollinger Zone: Historical estimate of 422,350 short tons grading 0.71% Ni and 0.42% Cu
  • Cominco Zone: Historical estimate of 130,000 short tons grading 0.73% Ni and 0.68% Cu
  • 10 km long exploration corridor open along strike and depth
  • Notable gold intercepts: 6.37 g/t Au over 8.55m, 6.17 g/t Au over 3.0m, 10.39 g/t Au over 3.1m
  • Surface grab samples: 8.98 g/t Au and 11.48 g/t Au (approx. 400m SE of Cominco zone)

Loyalist now owns 100% of one leased and 292 unpatented mining claims. Consideration to STLLR includes:

  • 10,000,000 Loyalist shares
  • 2.0% NSR royalty (Loyalist may buy back 1.0% for $1M)

Loveland Property Overview

The property lies in the Superior Province of the Eastern Canadian Shield, within the Abitibi Greenstone Belt. Local geology includes mafic to intermediate volcanic flows intruded by feldspar porphyries and gabbro.

Mineralization includes chalcopyrite, pentlandite, and pyrrhotite — occurring within gabbro and at contacts with volcanic rocks.

The Hollinger Zone

Located along mafic-intrusive and volcanic contacts, this zone shows massive sulphide development. Historical drilling outlined a zone 125 m along strike and up to 240 m deep, remaining open down-plunge. Overburden ranges from 12–41 m.

The Cominco Zone

Discovered by Cominco in 1972 and expanded by Amador Gold between 2007-2010. Multiple drill holes confirmed high-grade nickel and copper with open potential. Example: LL08-05 intersected 1.0% Ni and 1.92% Cu over 2.70 m.

Gold Mineralization

Gold mineralization was found 300 m below Cominco. LL08-13 returned 6.37 g/t Au over 8.55 m. Surface grab samples revealed values up to 11.48 g/t Au in mineralized granodiorite.

Exploration Potential

A 10 km corridor between the Hollinger and Cominco zones shows high potential. Historical geophysics and untested targets remain. Hollinger’s historical resource (1974): 401,000 tonnes @ 0.71% Ni and 0.42% Cu (not NI 43-101 compliant).
