About Loyalist Exploration Limited

Loyalist Exploration Limited (CSE: PNGC) is a mineral exploration company focused on a "buy Timmins" strategy by acquiring and advancing high-potential gold and strategic metals projects in the prolific Timmins Mining Camp. The Company’s projects include:


  • Tully - A gold deposit ready for production permitting.
  • Loveland - A large property with historic nickel/copper resources and high-grade gold drill intercepts and surface showings.
  • Gold Rush - An early-stage gold/silver property with high-grade drill intercepts.

Loyalist is committed to responsible exploration practices and building strong relationships with local communities.
