About Loyalist Exploration

Company Profile

Loyalist Exploration is executing a focused strategy of acquiring and advancing high-potential gold and strategic metals projects in the prolific Timmins Mining Camp – a district that has produced over 70 million ounces of gold and is host to one of the world’s largest volcanogenic massive sulfide (VMS) deposits, the Kidd Mine.

About Loyalist Exploration Limited

Loyalist Exploration Limited (CSE: PNGC) is a mineral exploration company focused on a "buy Timmins" strategy. The Company recently acquired the Loveland and Gold Rush Projects and has signed a LOI to acquire the Tully gold project.
