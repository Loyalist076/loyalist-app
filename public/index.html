"BUY TIMMINS, MINE TIMMINS"

Loyalist Exploration is executing a focused strategy of acquiring and advancing high-potential gold and strategic metals projects in the prolific Timmins Mining Camp – a district that has produced over 70 million ounces of gold and is host to one of the world’s largest volcanogenic massive sulfide (VMS) deposits, the Kidd Creek Mine.

Who We Are

Loyalist (CSE: PNGC) is a mineral development and exploration company focused on a “Buy Timmins, Mine Timmins" strategy by acquiring and advancing to production, high-potential gold and strategic metals projects in the prolific Timmins Mining Camp. The Company’s projects include:

  • Tully - A gold deposit ready for production permitting.
  • Desantis - Multiple gold deposits, close to Timmins and along the prolific Destor-Porcupine Deformation Zone.
  • Loveland - A large property with historic nickel/copper resources and high-grade gold drill intercepts and surface showings.
  • Gold Rush - An early-stage gold/silver property with high-grade drill intercepts.

Loyalist is committed to focused project development and building strong relationships with local communities.
